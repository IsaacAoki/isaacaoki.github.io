Direction B

Margin rail

Helix compressed into a narrow rail down the left edge. One honest reading column beside it; works reach out to the rail on short leaders.

For

The only direction where long prose stays comfortable. Rotation is always in peripheral vision, never competing with the words. Trivial to make responsive.

Against

Reads as an ornament stuck to the side rather than the thing the site is made of. Squeezed to 120 px, the depth cues barely register.
